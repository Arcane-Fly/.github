# Braden Group

Braden Group is the trading name of Braden Pty Ltd, based in Western Australia. We build workforce management software and provide supported consulting for Group Training Organisations, registered training organisations, labour hire, and recruitment providers.

Our work includes software for apprenticeships, traineeships, recruitment, and related business workflows, alongside custom SaaS development and workflow automation. We also publish [independent research](https://www.braden.com.au/research).

Explore our [website](https://www.braden.com.au/) or [contact us](https://www.braden.com.au/contact). For general enquiries, email [info@braden.com.au](mailto:info@braden.com.au).
